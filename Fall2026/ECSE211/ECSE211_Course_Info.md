## Learning Outcomes
1. Translate client needs into design requirements
2. Design and iteratively improve solutions
3. Test solutions against requirements
4. Apply project management to track human & material resources
5. Communicate design decisions through reports & presentations
6. Identify equity issues in design decisions
7. Reflect on the engineering design process

- **If you score below 50% on BOTH midterms, you fail the course regardless of your overall average.** (i.e. you must pass at least one midterm.)
- Grades after any Midterm 3 replacement are used for this requirement.
- **Coding Project track:** project grade is **capped at 50%** unless you attend the code review and show sufficient understanding.

## Assessment Details
### Assignments
- 3 handwritten assignments, 3% each, open ~1 week. Mainly midterm practice, graded on completion.
- Can work with classmates, but each student submits their own handwritten copy.
- Video solutions posted after deadline; can then book a **15-min TA meeting** on one specific problem.
- **Late assignments generally not accepted once solutions are released.**

### Midterms
- Two required, in person, closed book, 30% each by default.
- At least half = definitions & straightforward applications; rest = proofs and harder algorithm design.
- **Optional Midterm 3 (Dec 2, 6–8pm):** fully replaces Midterm 1 or 2 — **even if the new grade is lower.**

### Network Flow Proficiency Check
- Dec 1, first 30 min of class, 6%. Fundamentals & straightforward applications of network flow.

### Optional Mini-Interviews (~15 min each, 2% each)
- **Mini 1:** you choose the problem from a list given in advance.
- **Mini 2:** TA chooses the problem from a list given in advance.
- **Mini 3:** unseen problem.

### Final Assessment — Choose One Track (25%)
**Coding Project**
- Large Java project, graded mostly by automated tests (correctness + efficiency).
- Due **Dec 15**; mandatory 15-min in-person code review **Dec 16–18** (explain code, design choices, correctness/efficiency).
- Skip/fail the review → grade capped at 50%.

**Technical Interview**
- 45-min dialogue-based assessment during the final exam period.
- 3 algorithmic problems: 2 from a list of ~25–30 provided in advance, 1 unseen.
- Expect questions on reasoning, alternatives, correctness, efficiency.

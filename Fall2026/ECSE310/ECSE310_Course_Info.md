## Course Outline / Topics
1. **Introduction**
2. **Basic Electronics for Computation** — transistors, interconnects, scaling
3. **Introduction to Thermodynamics** — basic concepts, heat sink, fridge, thermodynamic entropy
4. **From Statistical Entropy to Information Theory** — statistical entropy, Shannon's measure of information, entropy of continuous variables
5. **Fundamental Energy Limitation on Computation** — Maxwell's demon, physical model for information, Landauer's principle, reversible & adiabatic computing, speed and energy cost

## Reference Books
- *Feynman Lectures on Computation* — Feynman
- *Fundamentals of Physics* — Halliday & Resnick (thermodynamics chapters)
- *Elements of Information Theory* (2nd ed.) — Cover & Thomas, Ch. 1–2

## Learning Outcomes
1. Recognise ICT's contribution to global energy use and greenhouse gases
2. Describe sources of heat production in a computer
3. Quantitatively determine heat sink requirements for a simple computing system
4. Articulate the laws of thermodynamics and their implications for computers
5. Define/calculate Shannon's Measure of Information and relate it to computing & communication
6. Define and apply entropy measures for physical and computational systems
7. Distinguish reversible vs irreversible computing and relate to simple switch models
8. Explain Landauer's principle and the fundamental limits of computing
